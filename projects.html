Ongoing projects

Two funded programmes are running in the lab, both centred on Curcuma caesia (black turmeric) — an endangered medicinal species of eastern India with no published reference genome.

Active

Whole Genome Sequencing and Evolutionary Insights of the Endangered Medicinal Plant Curcuma caesia (Black Turmeric)

The lab's flagship programme. Curcuma caesia is an endangered rhizomatous medicinal species of eastern India with a documented pharmacological profile but no reference genome. The project will produce a chromosome-scale assembly, annotate the gene space with emphasis on secondary-metabolite biosynthesis, and place the species in evolutionary context within the Zingiberaceae.

Active

Transcriptome Profiling and Pathway Analysis of the Endangered Plant Species Curcuma caesia

A companion study to the genome programme. Tissue- and stage-specific RNA-seq is used to describe the expressed gene complement of Curcuma caesia and to reconstruct the pathways underlying its medicinally relevant metabolites. The transcript evidence also supports annotation of the reference assembly.
