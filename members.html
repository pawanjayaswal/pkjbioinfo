Principal Investigator

PJ

Dr Pawan Kumar Jayaswal

Assistant Professor · Bioinformatics

Ph.D. in Bioinformatics, with fifteen years in plant genomics across the National Institute for Plant Biotechnology in New Delhi, the University of Arizona and BAU Sabour. Work spans reference genome assembly, transcriptome analysis, comparative genomics of repeat elements, and the design of SNP genotyping arrays now used in breeding programmes.
