For students

Research positions and projects

Students interested in genome assembly, transcriptomics or comparative genomics are welcome to write. Include a CV and a short note on which research area interests you and what you have worked on so far.

For collaborators

Genome and transcriptome projects

We are interested in collaborations on crop and medicinal plant species, particularly those without a published reference assembly. Say what material you have and what sequencing, if any, has already been done.
