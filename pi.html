Principal Investigator

Dr Pawan Kumar Jayaswal

Assistant Professor, Department of Bioinformatics, College of Agricultural Biotechnology, Bihar Agricultural University, Sabour. Ph.D. in Bioinformatics.

Profile

Pawan Kumar Jayaswal is a plant genomicist whose work runs from raw sequencing reads to the evolutionary questions that finished assemblies make it possible to ask. He joined Bihar Agricultural University in November 2023, where he teaches on the B.Tech. Biotechnology programme and established the college's Bioinformatics Laboratory.

Before Sabour he spent thirteen years in plant genomics at the National Research Centre on Plant Biotechnology and its successor, the National Institute for Plant Biotechnology, in New Delhi. There he worked on the mango reference genome and transcriptome, the first draft of the pigeonpea genome, the draft jute genome, and the design of SNP genotyping arrays for rice, pigeonpea and mango — two of which the Indian Council of Agricultural Research recognised as technologies in 2024. In 2023 he worked as Bioinformatics Scientist IV at the University of Arizona on HiFi and Hi-C assembly for a range of plant species.

His doctoral work, on conserved genes and repeat elements across crop plants, remains the backbone of the lab's comparative genomics line. His current programme applies the same assembly and annotation methods to Curcuma caesia, an endangered medicinal species that has not previously had a reference genome.

He has served as a resource person for Next Generation Sequencing and its applications in plant sciences under the ICAR Centre for Advanced Faculty Training in Plant Biotechnology in 2017, 2019 and 2022.
